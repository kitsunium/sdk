# internal/service/app/consensus/raft/

## Purpose

The step function of ADR 0152 (D15): no goroutine, no I/O, no clock. Every
decision of the protocol is here; every effect leaves through `Ready`.
Durability is an input (`StorageDone`), and the replies that depend on a
write leave in `Ready.AfterSync`.

## Do NOT

- Copy an appended entry's `Data` out of the message `Step` was handed: the entries' Data are the caller's gift (`Step`'s doc), and `appendNew` keeps them aliasing the frame the driver copied once out of the transport's buffer; an empty one is kept nil
- Iterate a map where the order reaches a message: a run must stay a pure function of its seed
- Append a session command below cluster version 4, from `ProposeSession`, `Propose` or a forward: a node of protocol 3 does not know the kind (`checkProposed`, PAYLOAD_VERSION_REFUSED; the raise to 4 waits for every member's `FlagProtocol4`, `announcedAt`), nor append a copy the leader knows: `sessionKnown` reads the latest entry of the session in its tail past the commit, then the table the committed entries left …
- Hand apply more than `MaxApplyLagBytes` past what the driver reports it holds (`SetApplyLag`), but one entry when it holds nothing, or anything in memory while it reads back entries handed by index: a follower slower to apply than its leader held everything it was behind by (#664, `mutApplyUnbounded`, `checkApplyBounded`; `mutApplyBehindRead`, `checkNothingHandedBehindARead`)
- Send a fetched batch as the store returned it: a store bounds its read by the payloads alone, and one append carries no more than `MaxBatchBytes` of its entries' encoding (`batchLen`, `wire.EntryLen`), the bound Config holds `MaxFrameBytes` above by 1 KiB
- Measure a peer's round trip on `rounds`: a round a majority confirmed leaves it at once, and the slower peer of three never measured one
- Read a heartbeat's `RejectHint` as the leader's last index without `FlagProtocol4` beside it, or send one below protocol 4: a leader of protocol 3 leaves it 0, and a follower of protocol 3 ignores it on a heartbeat, so the field needs no raise (#919, `heartbeatLast`, `hearLeader`)
- Acknowledge a snapshot install past its index (review H1, `mutSnapshotAckLastIndex`)
- Append, acknowledge or skip an entry kind `Known` refuses: report it in `Ready.Unsupported` (review M3)
- Adopt a snapshot's cluster version above what the node speaks, at a start or an install: report it in `Ready.Unsupported` and leave the version as it was (`adoptClusterVersion`, `refusesClusterVersion`), and refuse such an offer VERSION_UNSUPPORTED in `CheckOffer`
- Admit a forwarded command without the bounds of a local one (`admitForward`, review M2)
- Answer a past index from the engine state as it stands: `EngineAt` reads the records `noteEngine` keeps per committed engine entry, folded into the base by `Compact` and reset by `InstallSnapshot`
- Propose a forward each time it is delivered: the TCP transport resends a batch whose write failed on a reused connection (#377), so a frame can arrive twice
- Let a silent node campaign, on its election timer or a TimeoutNow: its candidacy is a vote for itself, and the silence grants none
- Campaign on a TimeoutNow while the driver holds its apply stalled (`SetApplyStalled`): a leader whose apply is stuck hands over (#554) and still acknowledges, and the transfer back to it made it lead almost all the time
- Clear a campaign's transfer issuer when it wins: `Status.HandedBy` names it while the term lasts, and the driver's watch of `PreferredLeaders` pauses after a transfer on it (#676, `TestTheStatusNamesTheLeaderThatHandedTheTermOver`)
- Admit a command while the no-space alarm is committed, locally or forwarded: `checkProposed` refuses it NO_SPACE and still admits the engine entries, the alarm's clearing among them (D17, #515, `mutNoSpaceAdmits`, `checkNoSpaceRefusesCommands`)
- Append to a peer an entry at or below `Config.Log.KeptIndex`: the entries a Recover kept carry the predecessor's identity, which only a snapshot of this lineage tells the peer, so `sendAppend` sends one (#507, `mutKeptAppended`, `checkKeptEntriesNeverAppended`)
- Answer, or take as contact, the appends and heartbeats of a leader this node refused for the term after `MaxChainRefusals` chain refusals: every rejection was contact for CheckQuorum and the trigger of an immediate resend, and the refusing majority never campaigned (#508, `mutRefusedLeadHeard`, `checkRefusedLeaderIsNotHeard`)
- Re-base an append on the commit without comparing the committed entries it carries, or count at once the refusals of a leader whose entry 1 differs from this founder's committed one: founders given different timings each commit their own entry 1, and the odd one refused every leader's chain, three refusals before the majority's leader first committed, cut off for the term with nothing said (#698)
- Match a held entry 1 of term 1 on its term alone, or take the chain past a held entry from the append rather than from this log: entry 1 is the one position no leader created, every founder writing its own from its InitialTiming, and a founder that kept its own refused every append, or, from index 0, appended the leader's entry 2 on top of it, its chain broken (#698)
- Judge entry 1 on its successor's chain alone (`foundingDivergence`) in any log but one that started holding entry 1 alone, committed (`fiatFounding`, what a data directory founded before Bootstrap left entry 1 uncommitted holds): a node whose entry 1 a leader committed stopped `BOOTSTRAP_REFUSED` on an entry 2 whose chain field rotted in the leader's memory (#698's review, …
- Refuse in silence a leader's verified founding entry 1 over another member list while this node's own is uncommitted: the divergence stop asked a committed entry, and such a founder refused every leader for good
- Report to a heartbeat any generation but 0 while the node holds nothing durably committed and no snapshot: its engine base is then its own InitialTiming, which no entry confirmed
- Admit a `ChangeSnapshotLimit` from any origin but the member it names (the authenticated origin `validateAdmin` holds, a forwarder or this leader), of a limit of 0 or below, or of the limit already recorded: only a member knows the limit it runs with, a 0 lifts every bound on what it is offered, and a repeat appends an entry for nothing
- Admit a DenyPin of this leader, or one that leaves no answering majority without its target, or withdraw the pin from the admission set before the change commits (#530, `mutDenyPinUnchecked`, `mutDenyPinAtAppend`)
- Judge a voter's isolation, for `Isolated` or `GuardRefusal`, other than by `reaches`, #603's own count: its zero `reach` is the guard's, and a bit set counts one more voucher (`reachUndenied`, a withdrawn pin counted back) or takes one bet on the leader less (`reachLeaderWhereKnown`, `reachNoBaseBet`) to tell the cause, never to admit or refuse a change (#773, #765, …
- Count a silent voter, one whose last answer carried `FlagTruncated` (`peerSilent`), in the strict reconfiguration check (`activeMajority`): it answers heartbeats but acknowledges no append, and a removal of a live voter admitted over voters {leader, silent} needed an acknowledgement that waited for a commit the removal blocked; nothing committed again, every node up (#790, …
- Report to a heartbeat a contract generation a restart would not rebuild, or campaign inside the node's own vote-refusal window: a lease granted on that report outlived the window after a crash (#521, `mutReportUnsyncedContract`, `mutCampaignInVoteWindow`)
- Size an acknowledgement of a generation the leader does not know as any window but none, or keep a node's own InitialTiming as a known contract once a snapshot named the cluster's (#522, `mutUnknownGenShortestKnown`, `mutOwnTimingKept`)
- Hold a lease under a contract with no drift budget in (0, 1), or run a founding contract without its maximum (#523, `mutLeaseWithoutDrift`, `mutFoundingAsWritten`)
- Move the lease only to a round sent strictly after the one it rests on
- Take a read's answer from any peer but the leader this node asked (#510, `mutReadRespAnyPeer`)
- Hand the driver the admission set at a commit that did not change it: every node paid a SetMembership, an address resolution and an Info line per committed entry (#531, `mutMembershipEveryCommit`, `checkMembershipPublishedOnChange`)
- Raise the cluster version to 2 while a member of the latest configuration was not heard in the term announcing it (FlagProtocol2), or to 3 while one was not heard announcing FlagProtocol3, or apply a raise above the version this node speaks: D18 refuses the raise while any member does not speak it (lot S3, #648, `mutRaiseUnannounced`, `checkRaise3Announced`)
- Add a learner over a key the configuration retired, or remove a member, or deny its pin, without retiring its key, at protocol 3: one key, one id for the cluster's whole life, or a node whose configuration still listed the removed member under the key refused the new one as leader (#648, `mutRetiredKeyAdmitted`, `mutKeyNotRetired`, `checkRetiredKeyRefused`)
- Keep the origin a proposer wrote in an engine entry: at protocol 2 the leader writes the authenticated sender, a forwarder or itself (#561, `mutOriginAsWritten`)
- Accept from a leader admitted by a `MsgLeaderRecord` alone anything but its appends and heartbeats, or take a record from a non-member, at an older term, for a known or retired ID, or while a leader is live (#542, `mutHintAnyKind`, `TestALeaderRecordIsBoundedAndEnds`)
- End a leader record while its leader is heard, or extend it on anything but an append or a heartbeat this node took as that leader's contact at its own term: the delay counted from the record cut a leader still heard, its snapshot stream included, and a node that hears its leader sends no PreVote that could bring a new record (#618, `mutHintEndsWhileHeard`, `checkVouchedLeaderAdmittedWhileHeard`, …
- End a silence under the leader of the node's own SilentTerm before the node's commit reaches the one the append carries, on an append the node refused, or on the commit of the snapshot it started from: that leader committed the entries the truncation dropped with the node's own acknowledgements, and the node voted again without them (#636, `mutSilenceEndsBeforeCommit`, `mutSilenceEndsOnARefusal`, …
- End a silence in the Ready whose entries meet its condition, or before the log is durable through the commit that met it: the driver removes SILENT as soon as a Ready says `SilenceEnded`, before the storage actor syncs that Ready's entries, and a crash during their sync restarted a voter without them
- Answer with a leader record a PreVote that does not carry FlagProtocol2, or wait for the cluster version 2 to send one: a node of protocol 1 does not know the kind, and the raise is refused while the stale voter the record is for stays unheard (#638, `mutRecordAtClusterVersion`, `mutRecordUnannounced`, `checkLeaderRecordFollowsTheAnnouncement`)
- Judge a PreVote behind the commit by its last index alone (`behindCommit`): a voter back with an uncommitted tail of an older term, longer than the commit, or a learner's question over one, lacks the committed entry as surely as a shorter log, and was never sent the record on a quiet cluster (#675, `mutRecordByIndexOnly`, `checkLeaderRecordReachesAnOldTail`, …
- Leave a node that may not campaign and hears no leader waiting for nothing, or let its question be a campaign: a learner, a silent voter or one with no table never sends the PreVote #542's record answers
- Leave a follower or a learner that hears its leader but cannot reach it with no way to learn where that leader listens: the TCP transport answers on the node's own outbound link, dialled to the address its configuration names, and a member down while `UpdateAddress` moved the leader answered the old address for as long as it led (#724)
- Rank a record of the leader's address below a latest configuration that is uncommitted and of a term older than the node's, or end the record on one: such a configuration ends a divergent tail the leader of the node's term never wrote and never commits (#675), and a node back with one past the cluster's commit refused every record and dialled its moved leader's old address for as long as the cluster stayed quiet …
- Answer a compaction at or below the base with nothing: the store may hold the log below it, back to the older retained snapshot less the trailing window (R6-02), once a restart started the step function at the newest one
- Hand the admission set without the index it is as of: a joining node's transport ranks it against its JOIN answer, as of the `AddLearner` it answers, and keeps the newer record of a member both list
- Start the commit at the persisted one alone under a state machine applied past it: a commit-only Ready is written lazily, a `DurableStateMachine` crashed past the persisted commit, and `EngineAt` at its applied index answered the engine state of that commit, which a cut recorded and the node restarted from it ran for good (#707)
- Promote a learner whose acknowledged match is below the configuration that admitted it (`admittedAt`: the first appended one that lists it, or the base when the base does): `PromoteLag` alone let a learner heard at match 0 pass while the commit was young, its joining stamp refused every vote, and once the leader failed the voters left elected no one (#722, `TestPromoteWaitsForTheLearnerToHoldItsAdmission`, …
- Resend a probing peer's probe only with entries: the probe a learner is sent as its admission commits reaches a joiner whose transport does not admit the leader yet, and is dropped
- Send a probe again, or move a replicating peer to probe, on a heartbeat answer that reports less than the oldest append in flight: on a link narrower than one batch per heartbeat interval the answer overtakes an append still crossing the data lane, and the copies, from match+1 at every answer, took some 95 % of the link and the follower never caught up (#767)
- Leave a silent peer in replicate on its FlagTruncated heartbeat answer: it acknowledges nothing, so its window never moves again, and with that window full it was sent only `probeBehind`'s empty append, never an entry of the leader's term, and stayed silent for as long as that leader led
- Leave a silent peer whose heartbeat answer reports this leader's whole log committed waiting for a write: truncated past its last entry, its truncation point lies past that log, `refeed` has nothing to send, and in a cluster that wrote nothing it stayed silent for as long as nobody wrote, one failure from no leader (#787)
- Lower a silence's truncation point in memory alone: the truncation that justifies it is durable, and a node restarted before the commit reached it came back on the old point with a log that no longer reached it, and stayed silent in a cluster that wrote nothing (#793)
- Keep a silent node's truncation point above a snapshot it installs over a log that held the snapshot's index under another term: the snapshot is committed, so no entry of that log from there on ever counted towards a commit (Log Matching), and a node brought onto the leader's snapshot over a tail of an older term kept a point past the leader's whole log, silent in a cluster that wrote nothing (#792)
- Drop a forward, or the answer to one, as an older term's message (`stepTerm`, `forwardKind`): both belong to the submission id, not to a term
- Answer an append, a heartbeat or a PreVote of an older term at this node's term, or take the later term of a message that is no request of that term (`answerOlderTerm`, `termRequestKind`, `stepLaterAnswer`, #1472)
- Answer an append or a heartbeat of an older term from a leader that does not announce protocol 5 at its own term, at the refuser's, or not at all (`refuseOlderLeader`, `announcesProtocol5`, #1472), or flag a `NOT_LEADER` refusal `FlagTruncated` (`truncatedReport`)
- Expect a node of a previous release to climb to a member's term faster than one election timeout a term (`gapBound`, #1472)
- Read the class between two nodes of a previous release as closed: it closes once one of the two runs this release (#1472)
- Take a leader of this release stepping down at its own term on a `NOT_LEADER` for a defect of the term rule: a frame it sent as a leader of protocol 4, delayed past its upgrade, is refused at the next term (#1472)
