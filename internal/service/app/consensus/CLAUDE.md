# internal/service/app/consensus/

## Purpose

The Raft engine of ADR 0152 and everything it ships: the event loop over the step function (`raft/`), storage, apply, snapshots, the segmented log and its recovery, the offline operations, observers, the replicated stream, backup, client sessions and the mTLS transport. The full inventory is the `Purpose` section of `internal/service/app/consensus/NOTES.md`.

## Do NOT

- Hand the machine a session command the apply's table did not admit, or decide it anywhere but on the apply goroutine
- Let the event loop wait on storage or a socket (D5b)
- Propose a stream's records concurrently, or hand its `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` back without the token
- Retry a failed sync
- Return a snapshot's STORAGE_FAILED without stopping the node
- Truncate a hole before every segment is classified (R8-02, R9-03)
- Fix a rule of `filelog.go`'s framing, batch protocol or recovery without fixing it in `internal/kernel/fs/seglog` too
- Serve a log recovery read without syncing it first
- Return a sync that failed at open without the STORAGE_FAILED markers
- Hold the log in memory to recover it
- Keep a segment's hard states past its scan when it is not the last
- Read the tail past the snapshot whole, or hand apply every committed entry
- Read the log whole in an offline operation, or from its first entry where the newest snapshot answers
- Read synced entries one pread of a header and one of a record at a time
- Copy what `Entries` returns, or let a store write over it
- Copy a received frame twice, or hand `Raft.Step` bytes the caller writes again
- Keep a slot per retained entry
- Sample a heap peak in the package's own process
- Compact the store past its end without the term
- Seal a segment again in recovery
- Restore a snapshot the machine has applied past, or install one whose freshness was not checked again after the transfer (review H3)
- Move raft onto an install it committed past while the machine restored it, or onto any install on a leader
- Sync once per queued Ready
- Probe a replicating peer whose heartbeat answer reports holding the oldest append in flight
- Write, sync, rename or remove a file of the data directory past its fault seam
- Hold `mu` across a batch's write and fdatasync
- Write a batch's end record before the rest of the batch is durable
- Bound a read of the store by the event loop's commit
- Truncate for `TruncateAndStaySilent` before the silence condition is durable, or persist a term below one a crashed run already wrote
- Restore a snapshot without answering the proposals it covers
- Wait for a proposal's answer on `p.done` and the context alone
- Run generation 1 on a node's own `InitialTiming`
- Answer a pinned snapshot's `Remove` with STORAGE_FAILED, or a failed unlink or directory sync with anything else
- Convert a length read off the wire or the disk to `int` before bounding it
- Keep `Recover`'s index at a persisted commit below the log's base
- Resume a spool across an empty chunk
- Create a directory of the data directory without `mkdirDurable`, or put the data directory on NFS
- Take a JOIN answer from an unpinned peer (`JoinConfig.Pins`, review M4), or admit a key two members share (review M5)
- Admit at the JOIN, once the cluster is at protocol 3, the key of a member removed or whose pin was denied, under any new id
- Extend the JOIN of `laneJoin` in place
- Leave the term of the last entry out of `Inspect`
- Cut `Recover`'s log back to the commit the survivor persisted
- Write `Recover`'s forced entry before `STOP-RECOVER` is durable, or remove the marker before the new stamp is
- Report a refused transfer as done on the node's own view that the target leads
- Fill a cut's metadata from the engine state standing now
- Publish a snapshot without `retain` after it
- Dial a sender link (control or data lane) without `boundUnacknowledged`, or drop the batch a reused connection failed
- Drop a snapshot trigger that falls while a cut is in flight
- Let a stopped node of a memory-network test hear what the cluster sent while it was down
- Note a compact record in replay and apply it only once the replay is done
- Release from the apply lag only the head of a batch a `BatchingStateMachine` applies
- End the TCP accept loop on anything but its listener's close
- Ship a snapshot of another lineage, or below a Recover's kept index, or install one that does not continue the node's own
- Keep the silence condition across `Recover`
- End a silence under the leader of the node's own SilentTerm before the node holds that leader's commit, or on an append it refused
- End a silence on disk before the entries that meet its condition are
- Hand the storage actor a truncation that lowers a silence's point before that point is on disk
- Turn a joining stamp into a member's on raft's commit alone
- Promote a joiner before it holds its admission
- Leave a silent voter that hears no leader with no way to reach one
- Take `TruncateAndStaySilent` on a member's directory when the truncated log and the snapshots would name no member
- Take `TruncateAndStaySilent` back to a configuration whose other members have all left since
- Take a member's `TruncateAndStaySilent` on its log alone, or blind
- Serve `laneMembership` to anyone but a member on its pinned key and slot, or read its answer without the member's pin, its certificate's slot, and the member listed …
- Refuse a JOIN on a held slot when the member holding it is the requester itself — id, incarnation, slot and key all equal
- Drop the answer to a forward because this node's term moved since, nor leave unanswered a forward that reaches its leader after that leader's term moved
- Hand the step function a forward window shorter than the transport's resend window, or take that window for the safety
- Forward a JOIN from a node that would answer it only once it applied the `AddLearner` itself
- Remove the snapshot a node with no table holds before its replacement is published, or an installed copy that did not restore on such a node
- Skip a snapshot of a version or codec this build does not know
- Refuse at the transport, unjudged, an offer whose metadata does not decode for its version, or skip such a `.meta` as one that rotted
- Offer a snapshot at cluster version 3 to a peer heard in the term whose last message announced no protocol 3: a release of protocol 2 cannot decode its version 3 …
- Take a snapshot's `ClusterVersion` without comparing it with `ProtocolMax`
- Stream again a snapshot a peer refused SNAPSHOT_CORRUPT without reading it back
- Let `Recover` name a voter other than the survivor
- Excuse an old voter from its fence because the new members list it, or accept beside the survivor a member under an old member's or a retired ID
- Judge the members of a `Recover` or a `RestoreSnapshot` one line at a time
- Remove anything in `WipeAndRejoin` before `STOP-WIPE` is durable, the stamp before the stores' removal is synced, or `STOP-WIPE` before the stamp and the other markers …
- Plan `Recover` from the log's bounds alone
- Publish a cut over `MaxSnapshotBytes` (or the smallest a member announces)
- Leave a `.tmp` a failed zero-fill wrote, or start a cut again right after one that failed without a storage failure
- Refuse an open because fallocate is missing, or preallocate a segment any other way than at its full size
- Refuse an open because `pwritev2(2)` or `RWF_DSYNC` is missing, or make a batch durable at any other points than its two
- Let the log grow past `MaxLogBytes` less `LogReserveBytes`, or the filesystem under `LogReserveBytes`, without the no-space alarm
- Keep `SnapshotTrailing` entries behind the compaction point once the log has no room for them, or accept a quota whose headroom over `LogReserveBytes` is two segments or …
- Compact only what the step function still holds
- Start the byte trigger from 0, or zero it for a cut that published nothing
- Hand raft a caller's buffer
- Number a read with a counter that restarts at 1: a leader may still hold a read of the previous incarnation, unconfirmed, and answer it later, at the commit index from …
- Overwrite a waiter
- Read a proposal's stage to answer a cancelled caller
- Report a token applied on its index alone
- Write entry 1 committed at `Bootstrap`
- Write the founding contract raw
- Cut a denied member before its DenyPin commits, or deny the pin of the last voter a quorum needs
- Take a log record whose crc holds but whose version this build does not write for a hole, or skip a record kind it does not know or a hard state of another length
- Keep a receive spool once an offer of another id is accepted, or past a reopen when it is not the newest, or read a spool whole to resume it
- Leave published a snapshot that failed to restore for a reason of its own once an older one restored and continues the log, or answer held for a snapshot past the …
- Refuse with LOG_CORRUPT a start whose fallback base does not continue the log
- End a streamed snapshot's pin on its own index
- Write a test whose setup needs a node to be offered a snapshot as a wait for the leader's log to pass a fixed index
- Hand a held snapshot stream its release with a bare channel send
- Run a timing contract whose `ElectionTimeoutMin` is not well above the disk's fdatasync latency
- Forward a removal of the leader without moving leadership first
- Start a transfer to a preferred node at the tick the previous one expired, to one not heard within `ElectionTimeoutMin`, or always to the first of `PreferredLeaders`
- Campaign on a `TimeoutNow` after handing the leadership over for a stuck apply, before the apply moves
- Start the watch of `PreferredLeaders` at the first tick of a leadership a transfer handed over
- Hand the leadership off to one voter only, or start a local transfer while raft still runs another
- Bound a hand-off try by `InitialTiming`, or by anything but the maximum election timeout of the contract raft runs on
- Answer a `Remove` of the leader submitted on it with the `NOT_LEADER` of its own hand-off
- Leave a peer's series at their last value once it is no longer published, or keep a departed member's certificate in `peerExpiry`
- Leave a series D13 names unpublished
- Trace a Propose from submission to apply on the follower alone, or set `FlagTrace` on a forward before the committed cluster version is 4: the span recorded `applied` …
- Report a follower Ready on its apply alone and say nothing of how far its log trails the leader's
- Answer `Membership()` on the event loop, or leave a product to poll it for a configuration change
- Bound an election in a test by a fixed number of split votes
- Restore a backup whose tail has not been checked against the chain, or read its metadata from anywhere but the export
- Decode an offline restore with flate when the expected metadata names another codec
- Resume a durable machine past its snapshot without the term of its position, or cut, offer, send or restore a snapshot at term 0 above index 0: the next start truncated …
- Start the step function at the persisted commit under a durable machine applied past it, or cut past the step function's commit
- Trust a version 1 `.meta` whose id does not name its position, or take an acknowledgement past the leader's last index as match
- Refuse, skip or write a version 2 `SnapshotMeta`
- Write a format of protocol version 2 before the committed cluster version is 2: the version 2 snapshot meta (CRC-32C, #546), the admin payload with Origin (#561) and the …
- Commit an administrative entry with an origin the forwarder wrote, or append one as leader without a Warn naming its origin (#561, …
- Admit a sender at the memory double that its receiver's configuration does not list when the test turns the admission model on (`MemoryNetwork.admission`)
- Leave a voter that came back behind a leader its configuration does not list with no way to hear it
- Gate the leader record on the cluster version
- Leave a learner whose configuration does not list the leader with no way to hear it
- Leave a member that hears its leader but cannot reach it at the address its configuration names with no way to learn the committed one
- Drop a batch the node's own link to a member cannot carry while that member's own connection to the node is open
- Trust a predecessor's token by its index above the identity's AdoptedFrom once its term can no longer be read
- Write anything, open included, before the STORAGE_FAILED markers of the current boot are read
- Open a data directory whose boot-scoped marker directory the process cannot write
- Write on an outbound lane without a reader on it, or after its key left the admission
- Keep one set of callbacks on a transport that several `Node`s share, or hand a frame or an offer to a group it does not name
- Install a JOIN answer in the engine's admission group, or send a snapshot whose configuration does not list the leader
- Take a member the JOIN answer and the engine's configuration both list from the map's iteration
- Keep a joiner on the JOIN answer it got first while it hears no leader
- Accept a `PeerQueueBytes` below `MaxFrameBytes`, or drop without a word a frame larger than the whole queue
- Refuse without a word a frame a member announces above the bound its lane is read under
- Read a snapshot offer under `MaxControlFrameBytes`, or end a refused one without a line
- Report a leader known from heartbeats alone
- Let a learner raise the cluster-wide no-space alarm
- Write a caller's writer on the apply goroutine
- Hold a stream's pin past its peer's `pinBound` after its last byte, or once the log is tight
- End a streamed snapshot's pin at a bound fixed from its stream's end

- Publish an install's applied position before its snapshot is recorded as the node's newest
- Feed the log to a learner whose announced limit the newest snapshot is over

- Cut again at an index whose snapshot is published
- Read, rename or remove a file of the snapshot directory outside the store's lock

- Judge a voter that no longer answers on the configuration the leader last heard it at
- Count the leader as a stale voter's voucher when that voter knows it as a learner only
- Count as a stale voter's voucher a member under another key than the non-zero one that voter pins for it, the leader included
