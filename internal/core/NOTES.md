| `data/queue/` | asynchronous DURABLE message-queue contract: the `Broker` port FROZEN at four methods (`Publish`/`Receive`/`Ack`/`Nack`), the `Handler` FUNC port, `MessageValue`/`DeliveryValue`/`LeaseValue`/`ReceiptValue`/`NackValue`/`DeadLetterValue`, `PolicyValue` + its shared guard (with `MaxRetryDelay`, a retry delay that grows — ADR 0151), and `DeadLetterReader`/`LeaseExtender`/`Waker` (+ `WakeValue`)/`Rejecter`/`DeadLetterManager`/`DeadLetterWriter`/`MessageReader`/`MessageWriter`/`Releaser` as the nine ADR 0039 siblings — the third lets an idle consumer sleep until a publication or a due instant instead of polling (ADR 0104), the fourth dead-letters a leased message at once and the fifth replays or deletes a dead letter (ADR 0151), the sixth writes one as another broker recorded it, the seventh and eighth list the live messages without a lease and write one as another broker recorded it (ADR 0178), the ninth hands a leased message back at once without counting its delivery — what `Consume` does with the messages a stop or a spent lease kept from their handler (kitsunium/sdk#474); the tenth, eleventh and twelfth (ADR 0183) nack after a delay the caller names (`DelayedNacker`, which `Consume` uses for a failure marked `RetryAfter`), publish a message no consumer receives before an instant (`DelayedPublisher`), and publish at most once per key over a window (`OncePublisher`); the thirteenth to nineteenth (ADR 0054 and ADR 0151, amended 2026-11-02) act on a whole queue — `MessageLister` (an iterator over the live messages), `Counter` (`StatsValue`, no payload read), `Purger` (`PurgeValue`), `DeadLetterPager` (the dead letters after a cursor, in the one order of the identifiers), `DeadLetterBulkManager` (replay or purge in bulk), `BatchPublisher` (`PublishMany`) and `Acker` (`AckMany`, one `AckOutcome` per receipt) —, beside `PolicyValue.MaxAge`, `DeadLetterRetention`, `RetryJitter`, `RetryJitterMode` and the handler's `Acknowledged`; it is the right-hand column of ADR 0053's frontier table, and the routing key is a NAME rather than a Go type precisely because a type has no identity in another process. Delivery is AT-LEAST-ONCE and the type says so: `Deliveries` counts from 1 on every delivery.
